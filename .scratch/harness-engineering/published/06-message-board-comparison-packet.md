## Parent

[Spec: trustworthy offline engineering evidence](https://github.com/matheusht/redteam-harness/issues/106)

## What to build

A reviewer can inspect three benign document cases and a fair communication protocol that tests whether discovery or late corrections through a board improve on a capable direct-handoff baseline.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Prepare three short document packets: complete sources, complementary evidence and a scheduled late correction. Include one stale or unsupported notice. Keep source access equal across arrangements and the answer key outside worker inputs.
- [ ] Specify serial complete-source work, parallel direct handoff with a curated packet, and the identical parallel arrangement plus a bounded task-local feed. Freeze model/configuration, total budget, assignments, correction timing and grading rules before execution. Externally scheduled observations/corrections reach the serial worker, direct-handoff coordinator and board arrangement at the same release point; count preparation cost.
- [ ] Require short independent initial observations before shared conclusions in both parallel arrangements. Fix the grader's reference assessment first, then grade anonymized designated final outputs while withholding treatment labels, peer discussion, worker self-grades and other cases' answers.
- [ ] Bound the feed in advance, initially at no more than five short notices per packet. Notices name task, author, source version, observation, applicability and uncertainty; a correction identifies its predecessor.
- [ ] Define separate observations for retained, delivered/read opportunity, used, correct and useful. A model's acknowledgment is not proof of comprehension. Preserve source-check and correction timelines.
- [ ] Count board reads, posting, curation, coordinator work, failed lookups, repeated analysis and human repair within the shared account. Necessary independent verification is not classified as waste.
- [ ] Validate complete and deliberately contaminated/incomplete illustrative accounts through the study-report boundary. Cross-case answers, extra source access or extra effective budget invalidate the comparison; affected assigned cases remain visible.
- [ ] The mechanism pilot may advance only when a validated notice explains an improvement over directed handoff within the same budget, with no new correctness, provenance or isolation failure. Equivalent complete results at equal or less effort favor the simpler baseline. Adoption requires a later repeated paired study with a predeclared practical benefit threshold.
- [ ] Keep all accounts clearly synthetic and the agent experiment unexecuted. Build no board service, shared belief store, event bus, embedding index, task dispatcher or automated cross-case memory promotion.
