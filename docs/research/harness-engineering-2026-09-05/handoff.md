## Accepted message-board comparison

The three arrangements are a single worker with complete sources, complementary workers using direct handoffs and a curated packet, and the identical parallel arrangement with a bounded task-local board. Start with complete-source, complementary-evidence and late-correction document packets. Include a stale/unsupported notice. Keep source access, model/configuration, total budget, scheduled observation release and grading rules equivalent.

Count preparation, coordination, messages, board reads, curation, failed lookups and human repair. Preserve independent initial observations. Fix the reference assessment before grading anonymized final outputs; withhold treatment labels, peer discussion, self-grades and other cases' answers. A notice being retained or delivered does not prove use, correctness or benefit.

The initial packet is a mechanism check. A larger repeated pilot is justified only when a validated notice explains an improvement over a capable direct-handoff baseline within the same budget without a new correctness, provenance or isolation failure. Adoption additionally needs a predeclared practical benefit threshold and repeated paired evidence. The simpler baseline remains preferred when it gives equivalent complete output with equal or less effort.
