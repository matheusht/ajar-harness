## Engineering evolution

The October 4 and 13 planning explored deeper research inside the queue/broker/kernel.
The October 18 migration study selected a separate native candidate and conditional
retirement of its unnecessary default process. The published
[migration map](https://github.com/matheusht/redteam-harness/issues/152) and
[specification](https://github.com/matheusht/redteam-harness/issues/156) retain that
planning context. Later operator implementation authorization is recorded in the
[cutover contract](../agents/native-cutover-contract.md).

Read earlier packages as dated sources. Transfer their useful properties, such as
authority, evidence, recovery, review and history, without treating every predecessor
mechanism as an architectural requirement. A process can retire only after its actual callers
and retained obligations are accounted for. The documentation consolidation imports
the durable conclusions; it does not copy the dirty working research tree or alter
its source records.

## Research lessons and limits

The [retained research](native-session-source-review-2026-10.md) graded three questions separately: whether a reported effect
was supported, who performed the research, and whether a particular tool or harness
component caused an improvement. An advisory or patch can support the first without
settling the other two. Assistance that a publication does not describe is unknown.

| Source family discussed | Useful lesson from the retained review | Limit to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PwnEscape](https://pwn.ai/blog/pwnescape) and [KVM escape](https://pwn.ai/blog/kvmescape) | Better instruments and measured feedback can overturn a failed explanation. Keep lab conditions and independently corroborated effects explicit. | Public successes do not expose a complete intervention history or isolate the benefit of orchestration/memory. |
| [Veria / XRP](https://verialabs.com/blog/biggest-hack-in-crypto-history/) | Preserve bounded supported results and conflicting external judgments; partial work need not have standalone final impact. | The receiving team's acceptance of a bounded result does not establish unaided discovery or a proprietary component's contribution. |
| [Hacktron incident reconstruction](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/here-is-how-openai-model-hacked-huggingface), [later primary analysis](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-incident-and-the-road-ahead/), and [independent study](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/) | Later primary evidence can change a reconstructed timeline. Preserve actual execution and effect evidence separately from displayed tool calls. | A reconstruction is not a complete original trace; a prepared evaluation does not establish general ordinary-agent reliability. |
| [Hacktron browser experiment](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/watching-gpt-55-sol-ultra-write-a-chrome-exploit-exploit-development-as-we-know-it-is-over) | Files and saved outputs can carry detail across context summaries; delegation is a serious option. | Supplied security changes, model changes, and compute differences prevent an equal-resource attribution to delegation or a separate memory service. |
| [Quarkslab / FreeRDP](https://blog.quarkslab.com/from-ai-agents-to-rce-building-a-vulnerability-research-workflow.html) | Source tools, slices, and instruments can expose useful conditions. Reusable stages can be entered selectively. | Human lead selection and repairs matter; the record does not establish agent-only completion or the need for a mandatory stage graph. |
| [V12](https://v12.sh/blog) and [its benchmark](https://v12.sh/benchmarks) | Whole-system context and faithful hardware/provider semantics matter. Specialist systems are serious alternatives. | Report-text matches differ from executed effects. Published results do not isolate components or establish unaided discovery and false-positive rates. |
| [Cantina / Apex](https://www.cantina.security/blog/ai-vs-human-penetration-test-benchmark) | Sustained work and specialized workers deserve serious tests. Account for environment, validation, and human effort. | A fitted search ceiling does not prove target exhaustion. Published counts do not establish a matched whole-system advantage or prospective general autonomy. |
| [Naptime](https://projectzero.google/2024/06/project-naptime.html), [Big Sleep](https://projectzero.google/2024/10/from-naptime-to-big-sleep.html), and [Aardvark](https://openai.com/index/introducing-aardvark/) | Interactive feedback, usable build/source tools, and faithful outcome checks are material. Measure supplied-lead work and setup repair separately. | These records do not establish that a new scheduler, fixed role system, or mandatory stage graph is needed here. |

The review supports testing a capable native baseline, not assuming its sufficiency.
Selected successful write-ups omit the failed-run denominator and often change
model, tools, information, or compute together. Treat architectural explanations as
hypotheses until an appropriate comparison separates them. Account for preparation,
human hints/repairs, reviewers, delegates, resumed contexts, and unknown spend.
