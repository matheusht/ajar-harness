# rounds.md — approach-family registry, primitive registry, round ledger

The three live artifacts required by `engine/multi-round-orchestration.md`. Append-only.

Route statuses: `unexplored` · `active` · `candidate` · `held` · `blocked` · `exhausted` ·
`confirmed` · `real-but-capped` · `refuted` · `real-but-not-reportable`.

**`blocked` records the specific missing mechanism.** Reopen only on a materially new construction —
a different bypass, a new pairing, a new reachable path, a new sink. Rewording does not reopen a
route. **`held` is not `exhausted`.** One failed construction does not close a mechanism.
