# E1-10 — tps_fee_recipients + paid-witnessing earnings chain

Pinned `a7eb776afc68ed4bed05ba554f7f1e95f5d49b44`. Escalation lane from round E1 (cascading/

## Verdict (one line)

**CAPPED — no escalation.** The tps-fee lifecycle is conserved: the declared fee is burned from the
unit's inputs, the settlement delta is the only balance movement, and recipients are constrained to
the author set. No vector enriches a chosen recipient, redirects the F4 poisoning debit, or creates
value beyond dust-level witness rounding (real, bounded, zero-mean, benefits only the OP list).

## 1. The declared fee lifecycle (full accounting)

1. **Declared fee is BURNED from the unit's inputs at validation** — the balance equation
   `total_input === total_output + headers_commission + payload_commission + oversize_fee + tps_fee
   + burn_fee + vote_count_fee` (validation.js:2651). The declared tps_fee is neither an output nor
   a transfer — it is absorbed by the transaction. The author must FUND it from inputs
   (validation.js:2649-2653).
2. **The ONLY tps_fees_balances writes are the settlement deltas** (storage.js:1261,
   `REPLACE INTO tps_fees_balances ... tps_fees_balance + tps_fees_delta`) where
   `total_tps_fees_delta = declared - final` (storage.js:1252). Grep-verified: no other write site
   in writer.js/validation.js/main_chain.js/storage.js.
3. **Recipients are author-constrained**: `getTpsFeeRecipients` (storage.js:1458-1467) — if ANY
   recipient is not an author, override to `{author[0]: 100}`. You cannot name a third party as a
   recipient of your unit's fee delta.
4. **The delta is split by the recipients' shares** (storage.js:1255-1261): each recipient's balance
   += floor(delta × share / 100). Shares sum to 100 (validated — validation.js:1100-1124 H3 in the
   round-0 f16 report).

### Conservation argument (why no money creation in the fee path)
- Declared D burned from inputs. Settlement credits/debits (D − F) to the recipients (authors).
- If D > F (overpayment — which the 10× estimation multiplier forces at minimum-declared units):
  the author receives (D − F) back as a tps_fees_balance CREDIT. The balance is a credit ledger
  against FUTURE declared fees (validation.js:1087 — it offsets the min-fee gate), NOT withdrawable
  money. Net lifetime burn per author ≈ Σ final fees. Conserved.
- If D < F (the F4 poisoning direction): the recipients (authors) are DEBITED (F − D). The debit
  hits only the author set — the attacker cannot redirect it to a victim (override at
  storage.js:1464-1466 requires recipients to be authors; a victim cannot be made a co-author
  without their key).
- Multi-authored units: the credit/debit is split per the recipients map (shares may be unequal).
  The burn came from the unit's inputs collectively. An author contributing no inputs but holding a
  share receives credit only when D > F — which requires the OTHER authors to overpay. No
  cross-party extraction: the credits stay within the author set and are not withdrawable.

## 2. The estimation-multiplier asymmetry (10×) — sender-side, conserved

- `getLocalTpsFee` (storage.js:1332-1340) applies `tps_fee_multiplier` (10) to the estimated fee;
  `getFinalTpsFee` (storage.js:1234) does not. A unit declaring the minimum estimated fee therefore
  declares ~10× the expected settlement → systematic overpayment → settlement credits the
  difference back to the author's balance. The churn is sender-side and conserved; it does not
  enrich a chosen recipient (the credit stays with the authors).
- **No new finding beyond the f16-T2 multiplier-inflation observation** (already recorded as
  below-floor, HN-5).

## 3. F4 (tps-fee poisoning) interaction — consistent, no redirect

- The poisoning debit hits the unit's RECIPIENTS (the authors). In a flood window, each author's
  own units debit their own balance — the F4 scatter as documented. The attacker cannot name a
  victim as a recipient (override). The `bFromOP` min-fee exception (validation.js:1092) applies
  only to OP-authored units (governance-controlled list — economic/governance attacks are out of
  scope per program-policy).
- **Marginal contribution:** F4's severity and blast radius are unchanged by the recipient
  mechanics.

## 4. Witness-earnings rounding — real dust, zero-mean, capped

- `paid_witnessing.js:162` (RAM path): `amountsByAddress[v.address] += Math.round(
  objUnit.payload_commission / countPaidWitnesses[v.unit])` — per-witness half-up rounding of the
  split; the SQL branch uses `ROUND(1.0*payload_commission/count_paid_witnesses)`
  (paid_witnessing.js:173) with a RAM-vs-DB cross-check (`throwError` if unequal,
  paid_witnessing.js:182-185).
- **Measured (real Math.round semantics):** the total paid to witnesses can EXCEED the burned
  commission (comm=5,count=2 → paid 6, +1 created) OR fall short (comm=7,count=3 → 6, −1;
  comm=1,count=12 → 0, −1). The direction depends on commission mod count — **zero-mean over
  time**, bounded at <count units per witnessed unit (≤11 base units with 12 witnesses).
- **Who benefits:** the MC-unit-designated witnesses (the OP list — readMcUnitWitnesses,
  paid_witnessing.js:196-210). An attacker cannot become a witness without the governance vote
  (out of scope).
- **Verdict:** real-but-dust. Not attacker-selectable, not bounded above by the commission size
  (the rounding is per-witness <1 unit), zero-mean. Ledger-integrity nit, informational at most.

## 5. Robustness nit — incomplete settlement on crash

- A crash mid-`updateTpsFees` (storage.js:1241-1263) writes some recipients' (address, mci) rows
  and not others; `getLastTpsFeesMci` (storage.js:1287-1290) tracks the last-written ROW (by
  rowid), not per-address completeness, so `updateMissingTpsFees` (storage.js:1268-1286) will not
  re-run the partially-settled mci — the addresses missing their row keep a stale balance
  permanently (a divergence between co-authors' balances). Crash prerequisite; not attacker
  exploitable; robustness note for the hardening backlog.
