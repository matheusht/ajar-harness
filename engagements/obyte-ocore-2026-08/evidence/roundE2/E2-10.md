# E2-10 — Estimation-asymmetry refund path (can the fee-credit refund create value?)

Pinned `a7eb776afc68ed4bed05ba554f7f1e95f5d49b44`. Escalation lane on the confirmed F4 finding
(settlement tps-fee) and the estimation/settlement asymmetry.

## Claim under test

The estimation applies `tps_fee_multiplier` (10) while settlement does not — so an author declares
a fee ~10× the settlement fee, and the settlement credits the difference to their
`tps_fees_balance`. Question: is that credit a refund (conserved), or an asymmetric gain (value
creation, or a convertible balance)?

## Verdict

**CAPPED — the asymmetry is a refund mechanism; the balance is fee-credit-only with no withdrawal
path; net cost equals the final fee in every flow; no asymmetric gain.**

## Mechanism (source)

1. **Estimation (validation requirement):** `getLocalTpsFee` (storage.js:1329-1341):
   `tps_fee_per_unit = round(tps_fee_multiplier(10) × base(10) × (exp(tps) − 1))`;
   total = `count_units × tps_fee_per_unit`. The min-fee gate (validation.js:1087) requires
   `tps_fees_balance + declared×share ≥ min×share`.
2. **Composer offset:** `declared = Math.max(round(tps_fee − balance/share), 0)`
   (composer.js:385-390, 629-630) — the balance reduces the DECLARED fee, not the input funding
   (the balance closure validation.js:2654-2656 requires the inputs to cover the full declared
   amount).
3. **Settlement:** `getFinalTpsFee` (storage.js:1228-1234) has NO multiplier;
   `updateTpsFees` (storage.js:1246-1261) applies `delta = (declared − final)` to each recipient's
   balance (`balance + delta`, REPLACE INTO). **storage.js:1261 is the ONLY writer of
   tps_fees_balances.**
4. **No withdrawal path:** every other reference to the balance is a READ that offsets a future
   declared fee (composer.js:385/629, light.js:638, storage.js:1425). No module converts the
   balance to outputs (empirically verified: `E2-10-WRITERS: []` across composer/writer/storage/
   wallet/light/headers_commission/paid_witnessing/mc_outputs/light_wallet).
5. **AA-trigger count variant:** estimation uses `1 + primary_triggers × max_aa_responses`
   (storage.js:1342-1350); settlement uses `1 + count_aa_responses` (storage.js:1249) — the actual.
   When actual < max, the over-declaration is refunded via the delta. Conserved.

## Empirical verification (real path, lab)

`evidence/lab/e2-10-run.js` — real storage/validation modules, synthetic author, seeded balance.

```
E2-10-RESULTS: compose at tps=30 (est), settle at tps=1 (final):
  estimation fee (mult 10): 1,068,647,458,152,350   <- declared (offset by balance)
  final fee:                                  17
  delta credited:              1,068,647,458,151,333
  balance: 1,000 -> 1,068,647,458,152,333
  accounting.net_cost:              17               <- equals the FINAL fee
  accounting.net_cost_equals_final: true
E2-10-WRITERS: []   <- no code path writes the balance except the settlement delta
```

The huge balance credit is the author's OWN inputs returned in credit form (they funded the
declared 1.068e15 from inputs; the credit returns all but the final fee). Net cost = final fee.

## Accounting proof (all flows net to −F)

| flow | declared | inputs funded | settlement delta | balance change | net cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stable rate, zero balance | 10F | 10F | +9F | +9F | F |
| stable rate, balance B | max(10F−B,0) | max(10F−B,0) | max(10F−B,0)−F | +max(10F−B,0)−F | F |
| rate drop 30→1 | 10F_high | 10F_high | 10F_high−F_low | +10F_high−F_low | F_low |
| AA triggers, actual<max | 1+N×10 × est | same | (1+actual)×F − declared | negative | F |
| poisoned author (B<0) | 10F−B | 10F−B | 10F−B−F | +10F−B−F | F |

In every flow the author's inputs minus the balance change equals the final fee F. The balance is
a two-way credit ledger that absorbs settlement differences; it is never consumed and never
withdrawable.

## Marginal contribution

World without the refund: authors would permanently overpay the 10× estimation (a tax) — the
refund is the mechanism that keeps the ledger fair. The "gain" hypothesis adds nothing over the
world without the bug: the credit is the author's own funds in non-withdrawable credit form.

## Residuals (not findings)

1. **Rounding dust:** ceil at compose vs floor at settlement leaves per-unit dust — E2-11's lane.
2. **Large parked credit:** an author can park arbitrarily large balance (their own value) — usable
   only to offset future fees; also acts as an F4-flood shield (robustness, not a gain).
3. **MySQL watermark double-application** (ER-4 e5 / HN-15): the only value-leak hazard; sqlite
   immune, MySQL unsupported, not attacker-triggerable.
