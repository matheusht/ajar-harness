# E2-11 — WITNESS-ROUNDING DUST: bias, routing, and scale analysis

Pinned `a7eb776afc68ed4bed05ba554f7f1e95f5d49b44`. Escalation lane E2-11 (E1-10 residual).
Full persistence; every claim verified against the pinned source + exact-arithmetic measurements.

## Verdict (one line)

**CAPPED — no escalation.** The settlement-floor dust (`Math.floor(delta*share/100)`) always
**destroys** value, can never be routed to a chosen address, and is directionally biased (negative
deltas over-debit, positive deltas under-credit) but negligible against the F4 magnitudes. The
half-up roundings (paid-witnessing, headers commission) can **create** value, but their recipients
are either the OP list (not attacker-selectable) or the creator pays ~50× the max gain
(economically dead, quantified below). Correction to the record: E1-10's "zero-mean" is imprecise —
the settlement floor is sign-biased and the half-up sites have a creation bias.

## 1. Settlement floor (the brief's target) — cannot route; sign-biased; destroys

Measured (exact arithmetic, `Math.floor(D*share/100)` summed):

| shares | D=-3 | D=-2 | D=-1 | D=+1 | D=+2 | D=+3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | exact | exact | exact | exact | exact | exact |
| 50/50 | -4 (over 1) | -2 | **-2 (over 1)** | **0 (under 1)** | 2 | 2 (under 1) |
| 60/40 | -4 (over 1) | -3 (over 1) | **-2 (over 1)** | 0 (under 1) | 1 (under 1) | 2 (under 1) |
| 33/33/34 | -4 (over 1) | -3 (over 1) | **-3 (over 2)** | 0 (under 1) | 0 (under 2) | 1 (under 2) |
| 99/1 | -4 (over 1) | -3 (over 1) | **-2 (over 1)** | 0 (under 1) | 1 (under 1) | 2 (under 1) |

- **Routing impossible:** `Math.floor(x) ≤ x` for every recipient — no recipient ever receives more
  than its fair share; the dust is destroyed, never credited anywhere. The chosen-address
  accumulation hypothesis in the brief is **refuted**.
- **Sign bias (the real residual):** negative deltas (the F4 direction) are OVER-debited — D=-1 with
  33/33/34 applies **-3 (3× the true delta)**; positive deltas (refunds) are UNDER-credited. So the
  dust amplifies the F4 poisoning by up to (recipients - 1) units per unit, and shrinks refunds.
  Against F4's exp() magnitudes (≥1e14 units), the dust (<12 units with multi-author caps) is 13
  orders of magnitude smaller — negligible.
- **Scale:** N flooded units destroy O(N) units per recipient — a rounding leak of the fee system,
  at the attacker's own fee cost, with no routing benefit. Not meaningful.

## 2. Paid-witnessing half-up — creation real, beneficiaries not attacker-selectable

Measured: `Math.round(comm/count)` per witness can EXCEED the burned commission (comm=5,count=2 →
paid 6, **+1 created**; comm=12,count=8 → paid 16, **+4**; comm=5,count=10 → +5) or fall short
(comm=3,count=12 → paid 0, -3). Bounded < count units per witnessed unit (≤11 with 12 witnesses).
**Half-up bias:** Math.round rounds 0.5 up — the long-run mean is slightly positive (creation),
not zero (E1-10's "zero-mean" imprecision). Recipients are the MC-designated witnesses (OP list);
an attacker cannot join without the governance vote (out of scope). **Not exploitable.**

## 3. Headers-commission half-up — the closest thing to a value-creation primitive, economically dead

- The winner child (lowest sha1(child_unit + next_mc_unit), headers_commission.js:249-257) can be
  **self-authored and ground** (the attacker authors P with hc=A, authors C as P's child, grinds
  C's hash to win — no competition needed beyond the grind).
- The winner's recipients CAN be external attacker addresses; `Math.round(A*share/100)` half-up
  creates dust: A=5, shares 50/50 → 3+3=6 (+1); A=1, shares 50/50 → 1+1=2 (+100% on A).
- **Quantified cap (why it is dead):** the attacker's unit size S ≈ 50·N bytes for N recipients,
  and the **payload commission ≈ S** (validation.js:260 reconciles payloadSize ===
  payload_commission); the max creation ≈ N/2. For every N:

  | N recipients | size/commission | max dust | NET (dust - commission - hc) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2 | 100 | 1 | **-100** |
  | 10 | 500 | 5 | **-500** |
  | 100 | 5000 | 50 | **-5000** |
  | 200 | 10000 | 100 | **-10000** |
  | 1000 | 50000 | 500 | **-50000** |

  The commission alone is ~50× the max gain — **the attacker always loses ~49.5 units per
  recipient per round-trip**. The exponential oversize fee (storage.js:1137-1155, `size*exp(size/
  threshold-1)`; size 100000 → 8.1e8; Infinity past ~710×threshold) makes large-N constructions
  astronomically negative. **Economically dead by two orders of magnitude.**

## 4. Corrections to the record

1. E1-10's "zero-mean" dust: imprecise. The settlement floor is **sign-biased** (always destroys;
   negative over-debits up to 3× for |D|=1, positive under-credits). The half-up sites have a
   slight **creation** bias (Math.round rounds 0.5 up). The boundedness and non-exploitability
   claims hold.
2. The hc site's external-recipient routing is real but its creation ceiling (N/2 units) is
   dominated by the payload commission (≈50N) — the marginal contribution of the rounding to any
   attack is zero.

## 5. Residual / notes

- The settlement-floor dust is a conservation leak (value destroyed) bounded < 1 unit per recipient
  per unit — a ledger-integrity nit, informational. The F4 finding is unaffected.
- The hc recipient count has no explicit cap in validateHeadersCommissionRecipients — the unit size
  (MAX_UNIT_LENGTH=5e6) is the de facto cap; the oversize fee already prices large units
  exponentially. No change recommended beyond the existing hc-grind note (f16-T3).
