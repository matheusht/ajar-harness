# E2-9 — NATURAL-BURST F4: 20 units in one second (mass-claim pattern) — empirical

Pinned `a7eb776afc68ed4bed05ba554f7f1e95f5d49b44`. Two-node lab (hub:6611 + node:6612, real relay
protocol, real validation/save/stabilization/settlement). `tps_interval=1` (mainnet value).

## Verdict (one line)

**REFUTED — the natural-burst freeze does NOT occur.** A 20-unit burst settled with actual fees of
0–1 base units (settlement tps peaked at ~5.5, not the ~20 the theory assumes), and the author's
`tps_fees_balance` went POSITIVE (+10 base per unit, the 10× estimation-multiplier overpayment
refunded). LR-3's "single-digit GBYTE small-author freeze" is corrected: the honest composition
pipeline caps a natural burst at ~6 units/second, so the poisoning threshold (count≥~15 in a
1-second window) is unreachable without optimized attacker tooling — which is the F4 attack lane,
already economics-capped.

## Experiment

`evidence/lab/e29-run.js` (ROLE=node / ROLE=hub): deterministic synthetic genesis, one witness,

## Why the freeze does not occur (mechanism, with file:line)

1. **Composition throughput caps the natural burst rate.** Each honest compose+validate+save takes
   ~150 ms (measured: 20 units spanned ~3 s). A 1-second natural burst is ≤ ~6 units → tps ≤ 6 →
   fee ≤ 10*(e^6−1) ≈ 4,030 base. The poisoning needs tps ≥ ~15 (fee ≈ 3.3e6 base = 0.0033 GBYTE)
   or tps ≥ 30 (F4's 10.7%-of-supply figure) — both unreachable with honest composition.
2. **The settlement's elapsed denominator dilutes the rate.** `getFinalTps`
   (`storage.js:1174` `elapsed = (mc_ts − last_ball_ts) || 1`) uses the MC unit's `last_ball_unit`
   timestamp — the ball creation lags the burst, so the observed elapsed ≈ 7 s for a burst the
   composer posted in ~3 s. tps = count/elapsed ≈ 20/7 ≈ 2.86, not 20.
3. **The 10× estimation multiplier dominates.** `getLocalTpsFee` (estimation) applies
   `tps_fee_multiplier` (10); `getFinalTpsFee` (settlement) does not (`storage.js:1228-1234` vs
   `:1329-1339`). Declared (10 base) > final (0) for every burst unit → the delta is positive →
   the balance is CREDITED (+10/unit), never debited. The natural burst is a small net credit to
   the author, not a freeze.

## LR-3 correction

LR-3 predicted a "realistic natural damage" of a single-digit-GBYTE small-author freeze from a
sharp 20-unit spike. The empirical run refutes the premise: a 20-unit spike cannot land in one
second through the honest pipeline (throughput cap), and even the achieved spike settled at trivial
fees with a positive balance. The natural-burst framing of F4 (cascades.md Cascade 4, "a NATURAL
burst ... would freeze every author") is therefore **removed**: the mechanism requires the
ATTACKER's optimized parallel composition (the F4 attack lane), which remains economics-capped.

## Residual risks / notes

1. The node-side delivery had dependency-resolution stalls (unresolved parents; the node's DB only
   reached genesis+1 ball) — the settlement measurement is taken from the HUB's own full-node
   settlement path (identical code: `writer.saveJoint` → `advanceMcStability` →
   `storage.updateTpsFees`). The node-side path is not required for the fee-settlement question.
2. An attacker with optimized tooling (parallel pre-composition + direct broadcast, bypassing the
   per-unit local validate/save) can post faster than 6/s — that is the F4 attack lane, unchanged
   and still capped by the funding bound (inputs must cover the declared fee).
3. `tps_interval` is a governance parameter (`system_vars`): a vote lowering it to 0.1 (or a future
   base_tps_fee increase) would raise the natural-burst risk — noted for the governance lane
   (E2-14), not a live finding.
4. The waypoint actual fees (1) vs the logged final tps (3–5.5) mismatch suggests the settlement
   fee update and the `getFinalTps` debug call observe different cache states (the log call is
   `console.log` inside `getFinalTps`, the update uses the returned value at a slightly different
   time). Either way the fees are sub-GBYTE and the conclusion is unchanged.

## CORRECTION (2026-08-11, ER4 review) — evidence-quality note on the F4 refutation numbers

ER4-4 + ER4-2 independently found: the E2-9 and E4-2 real-network flood runs computed settlement
with tps_interval=60 in the in-memory cache (a lab-config artifact; the DB carries interval=1).
Their measured trivial fees (0-190 base; +181 credits) are therefore conservative in magnitude.
Independent re-derivation at the true interval=1: fees ~2 to 1,736 base, negative deltas,
self-frosting — still ~6 orders of magnitude below the F4 10.7%-of-supply figure, and the
elapsed-dilution (M2) + funding bound + repayable debt protect honest authors. **The demotion
direction survives; only the magnitude evidence was softened.** E3-10's S1-S3 arithmetic used
interval=1 (correct); its M1 "count=1 always" rationale was itself too strong (E2-9 produced
count=22 — a real lag state exists; the true protections are M2 + self-frosting + funding bound).
