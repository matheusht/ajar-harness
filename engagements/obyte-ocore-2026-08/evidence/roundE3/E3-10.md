# E3-10 — Exchange hot-wallet F4 exposure: REFUTED (two independent mechanisms)

**Pinned:** ocore `a7eb776afc68ed4bed05ba554f7f1e95f5d49b44`.
**Claim under test (from E2-15 + F4/obyte-2026-08-004):** an exchange hot-wallet address (a single

with count>=30 and elapsed=1 debits the exchange's `tps_fees_balance` by ~10.7% of the supply,

**Verdict: REFUTED — the exchange (and every honest author) is protected by two independent

## The two refutation mechanisms

### M1 — 1-MCI-at-a-time stabilization: every unit settles with count=1 (the count>=30 state is unreachable)
`advanceMcStability` stabilizes ONE MCI per pass ("// not trying next any more",
main_chain.js:540-543); the writer throws if a pass stabilizes more than one (writer.js:718/740).
Each unit therefore settles when the stability point reaches its mci, with the previous unit as
its last ball — `getFinalTps`'s window walk (storage.js:1178-1218) counts units with
`main_chain_index > last_ball_mci`, i.e. count=1. **Measured on the real storage path:** a
30-unit chain settling under the 1-at-a-time advance produces fee=17 at EVERY step (the count=1
fee: round(10*(exp(1)-1))=17), including the 30th unit. The count=30 state that r2's drive seeded
(last ball at mci 0, tip at mci 30 — synthesized, like p2's crash state) is what produced the
10.7%-of-supply number; the pinned stabilization never creates it.

### M2 — wall-clock elapsed dilution: even in a lag state, an honest unit's fee is trivial
Even IF the lag state existed (a future stabilization change), the exchange's unit is authored
with its own wall-clock timestamp (the composer sets `Math.round(Date.now()/1000)`), so its
settlement elapsed = (its ts − last_ball_ts) = the waypoint age (seconds), NOT 1. The equal-
timestamp trick (setting ts equal to the previous waypoint's) is only usable by the ATTACKER on
its OWN units. **Measured:** an exchange unit with ts = T+5 in the synthesized lag state (count=31
in the window) settles at **4,917 base units (~0.000005 GBYTE)** — six orders of magnitude below
the attacker's 1.07e14 — because elapsed=5 dilutes tps to 31/5≈6.2.

### Corroboration — the E2-9 real-path run
The real two-node lab burst (20 units, same wall-clock flow) settled with elapsed≈7s (the
ball-creation lag places `last_ball_unit`'s timestamp before the burst), tps≈3, fees 0-190 base,
balances monotonically INCREASING (+181, the 10x estimation multiplier credits), **no negative
balances anywhere**. The lag that produces a big count also produces a big elapsed for non-attacker
units — the correlation is structural, and the equal-timestamp trick breaks it only for the
attacker's own units (self-frosting, not a collateral freeze).

## CORRECTION (2026-08-11, ER4 review) — evidence-quality note on the F4 refutation numbers
