## Sweep 1 — responseVars limits: BOUNDED (no engine gap)

- `responseVars` (formula/evaluation.js:72, :1427 `assignField(responseVars, var_name, res)`) has
  **no explicit count/size cap** in aa_validation.js (grep: 0 hits for responseVars/response_vars/
  MAX_RESPONSE in aa_validation.js).
- **The bound:** responseVars are emitted into the AA's RESPONSE UNIT, which is a normal unit
  validated end-to-end: `validateAndSaveUnit` (aa_composer.js:1789) → `validation.validate` → the
  full unit pipeline → `MAX_MESSAGES_PER_UNIT=128` (constants.js:45) and `MAX_UNIT_LENGTH=5e6`
  (constants.js:59) apply. State vars separately capped at
  `MAX_STATE_VAR_NAME_LENGTH=128` / `MAX_STATE_VAR_VALUE_LENGTH=1024` (constants.js:65-66).
- **No unbounded response.** An oversized response unit is rejected by the standard unit caps; a
  huge responseVars payload is bounded by the unit size it must fit in. Deterministic across nodes.
- **Coverage note:** E2-6 (AA path on the network) + E3-8 (AA→AA chains) exercise the response
  path; the bounds are structural (unit caps), not author-dependent.

## Sweep 2 — AA storage-fee interplay with the F4 curve: NONE (fixed-price, conserved)

- The AA's byte balance is its base balance 1:1: `byte_balance = trigger_opts.assocBalances
  [address].base || 0` (aa_composer.js:479, :517) and `byte_balance += assocDeltas.base`
  (:577).
- Storage is priced at **1 byte = 1 base unit** (the byte balance must cover the storage size:
  `byte_balance < new_storage_size → bounce`, aa_composer.js:1556) — a FIXED price, **no
  interaction with the exponential tps-fee curve** (F4). The F4 curve prices unit authoring, not
  state storage.
- Accounting conservation verified by E1-11 (16/16 value shapes, 0 mismatches) and ER2-4.
- **No interplay to exploit.** An AA's state growth cannot be leveraged against the F4 curve and
  vice versa; the two fee systems are orthogonal.

## Sweep 3 — max_aa_responses fee interplay: CAPPED (responses are paid for on the trigger side)

- The settlement window count includes AA responses: `count = 1 + (objMcUnitProps
  .count_aa_responses || 0)` (getFinalTps, storage.js:1178-1218) and
  `count_units += count_primary_aa_triggers * max_aa_responses` (getCountUnitsPayingTpsFee,
  storage.js:1343-1347).
- AA responses carry NO tps_fee (validation.js:1054-1056: "tps_fee in AA response" rejected) —
  but the **trigger** that produces them is priced with the multiplier
  `count_units = 1 + triggers × max_responses` (the trigger's min/acceptable fee scales with its
  responses).
- **Conclusion:** inflating the settlement window with free AA responses is NOT cheaper than with
  normal units — the response cost is pre-paid through the trigger's fee multiplier. The F4 curve
  cannot be driven more cheaply via AA responses. (This confirms E3-9's lane direction; the
  empirical flood bound from E2-9/ER2-2 — composition throughput ~6/s — caps the rate regardless.)
