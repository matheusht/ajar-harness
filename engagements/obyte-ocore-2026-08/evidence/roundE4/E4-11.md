## 2. Field-notes lesson list (portable, abstract — newest first)

Format per the field-notes doctrine: **what we hit → what we changed → the portable rule.**

1. **A real-path drive can still be a synthesized-state error — including in our own finding.**
   The tps-fee poisoning (finding 004) was "confirmed" by driving the real storage functions with
   a seeded cache (r2), then the escalation phase refuted it: the 1-MCI-at-a-time stabilization
   makes every unit settle with count=1 (real-path fee=17 at every step, E3-10), and the
   real-network run corroborated (fees 0-190, balances monotonically increasing, E2-9). The
   seeded state (30 units in a window with equal timestamps) is unreachable. → Changed: finding
   004 demoted. **Rule: a PoC that seeds ledger state must prove the state is reachable — and a
   real-path drive of the settlement function is not a real-network proof; verify WHERE a
   settlement runs relative to ball creation (last_ball_mci) before believing any window-count
   claim.** (The p2 crash lesson, applied to our own record.)
2. **SQL-engine semantic claims must be executed, not assumed.** The `+sequence='good'` MySQL
   coercion claim (f2) was refuted by executing the exact predicate on sqlite/MariaDB/MySQL8/5.7:
   unary plus coerces only pure-integer strings; the predicate is correct on every supported
   engine; and `conf.js` marks MySQL unsupported. → Changed: the finding was closed at the
   refutation. **Rule: a documented coercion is a hypothesis; run the query on the actual engines
   before claiming a divergence.**
3. **Eval-side gates can lag validation-side disjunct migrations — audit BOTH sides of a rule
   boundary.** The validation-side gates (17+ sites) carry the `!hasBall && minRetrievableMci`
   disjunct; the evaluation-side gates (10+ sites in formula/evaluation.js) have none. The
   mechanism was confirmed (position-dependent acceptance divergence, real-path 7a/7b/7c) but the
   reachability died at stabilization. → Changed: recorded as a hardening item (HN-1). **Rule:
   when a migration adds a disjunct to one side of a rule boundary, grep the other side for the
   bare gate.**
4. **A blind no-new-family round can find what 16 directed families miss.** The device-layer
   scheduled throw (device.js:431-432, remote zero-cost wallet crash) was found only by the
   prior-free blind sweep (LR-4), not by any of the 16 directed families (which covered
   validation, main-chain, signature, definition, formula, assets, private payments, network,
   storage, AA, encoding, races, fail-open, composer, economics). → Changed: the family was added
   to the coverage; the candidate quarantined (adjacent surface). **Rule: a directed family
   portfolio plus one blind prior-free sweep is the coverage floor for a novel codebase.**
5. **The adversarial round corrects the engagement's own record.** The escalation phase refuted
   its own finding (004) and the R1 refuter refuted a triple-sourced "confirmed" crash (p2). →
   Changed: 004 demoted; the crash family closed. **Rule: the refuter/verify-of-verify
   discipline applies to the engagement's own conclusions, not just the target's behavior.**
