## The abstract-shape discipline (what was scrubbed vs retained)

- **Stripped:** target name (only "DAG distributed ledger core"), the pinned commit id, every
  unit/address/hash literal, evidence file paths (replaced by engagement-local labels F1/F3/F4 and
  surface names), any URL.
- **Retained:** mechanism shapes at file-level granularity ("a validation rule gated on the unit's
  position height", "the uniqueness key is the unit's own author"), the refutation trail (what
  LOOKED like a finding and what killed it — the false-positive corpus), the held-defense
  contrasts, and the reusable procedures (the disjunct-migration audit method, the settlement-
  ordering check).
- **Sanitization sweep (executed):** grep for base64/hash-shaped literals (≥40 chars), hex strings
  (≥40), URLs, and the pinned commit id across all six files → zero literal hits (the only text
  mentioning "base64" is the verification-note metadata itself).

## The casebook's teaching value (beyond the wins)

The strongest rows in the matrix are the REFUTATIONS, not the confirmations:
1. **F4 (self-refuted):** a plausible economic-DoS finding whose impact was killed by the real
   settlement ordering — the casebook records the synthesized-state error so future audits check
   reachability before believing a window-count claim.
2. **crash-family:** four real uncaught-throw sites, all unreachable — the state-machine reason is
   the lesson (resolution atomic with balling).
3. **s1-old-dag:** the migration-completeness audit (grep both sides of a rule boundary) found the
   incomplete disjuncts, and the stabilization trace killed the reachability — a two-step
   mechanism-vs-reachability template.
4. **mysql-divergence:** the "documented coercion" that execution refuted — the execute-before-
   believing lesson.
