## Why count=1 holds even with equal timestamps (source + measurement)
- Every unit's settlement runs AFTER its own mci is balled (stabilizeMci: markMcIndexStable →
  addBalls → updateTpsFees), so the last ball for the settling unit is its parent's ball (one MCI
  back) — `getFinalTps`'s ancestry walk (storage.js:1178-1218) skips everything ≤ last_ball_mci →
  **count=1 per unit**.
- The equal-timestamp construction makes `elapsed = (mc_ts − last_ball_ts) || 1` = 1, but with
  count=1 the fee is `round(10·(exp(1)−1))`-class (single digits), not exp(30).
- 1-MCI-at-a-time stabilization (writer.js:718/740 throws on >1 per pass) makes a 30-MCI ball lag
  structurally impossible — count≥30 is unreachable, for the attacker AND honest authors.
