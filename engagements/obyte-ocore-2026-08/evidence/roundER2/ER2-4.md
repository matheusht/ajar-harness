1. E2-5 same-author save-path (U3 UNIQUE rejection) not empirically demonstrated — single-node
   harness MC limitation (main_chain.js:197-198); statically certain. Full confirmation needs the
   two-node lab.
2. E2-13 R1 — kvstore-del-after-SQL crash window (HN-2 family): a coincident mid-archive crash
   leaves a kvstore joint with no sql row → `throw` at storage.js:92 on re-delivery → crash-loop
   persistence. Requires a coincident crash; not directly attacker-triggerable.
3. AA-specific logic keyed on `serial_number` alone could conflate duplicate-serial coins
   (E2-5 residual) — AA scope, requires issuer's own second key.
4. Composer-side float rounding for whale AA sums > 2^53 (E2-3 residue) — bounce or self-inflicted
   AA-accounting drift only; no cross-party value.
