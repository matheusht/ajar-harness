# Corpus — HackerOne dispositions / severity / state-history (non-prose axes)

> Backing corpus for the axes the [prose humanization pass](hackerone-genuine-human-corpus.md) did not
> mine: the program's own **disposition** (state/substate), **assigned severity**, and **state-history**.
> Built per `docs/research/hackerone-disposition-oracle-plan.md`. Grounds
> `skills/oracles/adjudication-oracle.md` "what does NOT count" (negative exemplars),
> `engine/chainability.md` angle-shapes (real disclosed chains, replacing the single-Cosmos-finding
> basis), and a `severity_rubric` cross-check.
>
> **Ground truth = the program's disposition (substate), never the researcher's self-claim** (plan §2
> rule 1). **Disclosed-set bias (rule 4):** disclosed reports skew resolved/bountied — bucket A
> (negatives) comes from a targeted widening hunt, NOT from a representative sample. Every IN row was
> independently re-fetched at hackerone.com/reports/<id>.json. Assembled deterministically from the
> run's adjudicated rows, not synth-authored.
>
> Disposition spread (verified IN): informative:5 · resolved:65 · duplicate:5 · not-applicable:5.
